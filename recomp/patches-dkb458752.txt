# The player's damped velocity update (main+13fad60; its eight callers are the player's movers, the wall and ceiling
# climbs among them) steps v = r^step * v + sum of r^i * target for i = 1 .. step: it adds the target once per whole
# 60 Hz frame. A step under 1.0 (any rate above 60 fps) adds nothing, so the climb velocity decays to 0 and DK stops on
# walls and ceilings (#201). runtime/frame_rate.c damped_sum (patch page +0x260) stands in for the fmov s12, #1.0 that
# bounds the loop: on a fractional step it puts the closed form of the sum in s9..s11 and +inf in s12, so the loop does
# not run; on a whole step it leaves s12 = 1.0 and the game's loop. The three movi that clear s9..s11 on the path that
# skips the loop become nops so the sum reaches the store. At the BL x0, x1 and s8 hold the mover, the target and r; the
# function saves LR in its prologue (main+13fad70), so the BL keeps its return.
